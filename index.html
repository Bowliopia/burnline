Stories from a better future.

Burnline Magazine is a new, independent publication that aims to publish imaginative solarpunk short fiction and art about a better future where we live alongside the natural world, not in contrast to it.

The first issue

The first issue of Burnline Magazine is coming soon! Have solarpunk fiction or art you'd like to see published? Submit it to us.

Our Point of View

The future doesn't have to be so grim.

Sci-fi often shows a future full of extraction and exploitation, but what if humanity actually gets it right? It's our goal to help creative people show us what a world with a harmony between technology, the environment, and people could look like.
